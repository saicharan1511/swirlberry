Sweet questions.
Simple answers.

A few things you might like to know before your next Swirlberry moment.

Ask Us Something Else ↗
How can I enquire about a party order?

Visit our Party & Bulk Orders page or select “Party and bulk orders” in the contact form. Share your event date, guest count, and dessert ideas.

Can I ask about allergens and ingredients?

Yes. Contact the team about a specific item before ordering. Ask about ingredients and preparation practices, including possible cross-contact.

Do I need to book before visiting?

Please contact the parlour to confirm seating arrangements. For larger groups or special occasions, enquire in advance.

How do I check delivery or takeaway availability?

Contact the team with your location, preferred items, and requested time to check available options.

Where can I share feedback?

Select “Feedback” in the contact form. Include the date of your visit and anything you would like the team to know.
