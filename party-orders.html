              How far ahead should I enquire?
              
            
            

              Enquire as early as possible, especially for large orders,
              custom cakes or busy dates. The required lead time depends
              on your order and must be confirmed by the team.
            

          


          

            
              Is there a minimum bulk order?
              
            
            

              Minimum quantities can vary by dessert and preparation
              requirements. Share your guest count so the team can
              confirm suitable options.
            

          


          

            
              Can I mix different flavors?
              
            
            

              Include your preferred flavors in your enquiry.
              Available combinations depend on the selected products,
              quantities and availability.
            

          


          

            
              Can you arrange delivery or event setup?
              
            
            

              Share your venue and timing requirements. Delivery,
              service areas, charges and any setup arrangements
              need individual confirmation.
            

          


          

            
              What about allergies and dietary requirements?
              
            
            

              Tell the team about all allergies and dietary needs before
              ordering. Ask about ingredients and cross-contact;
              suitability cannot be assumed.
            

          


          

            
              When is my order confirmed?
              
            
            

              Your enquiry is a starting point. An order is confirmed
              only after the team agrees to the details and you
              complete the stated confirmation requirements.
