Ask Us Anything ↗
How do I enquire about a service?

Select View Service, explore the available options and use its enquiry button. Tell us your date, quantity and preferences.

Can I personalise my order?

Yes. We can discuss flavours, portions and presentation. Custom requests depend on preparation time and availability.

Are the displayed prices final?

Prices are illustrative starting estimates. Your final quote depends on quantities, customisation and any delivery or staffing requirements.

Do you accommodate food allergies?

Please discuss allergies before ordering. Our kitchen handles milk, nuts, wheat and other allergens, so we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Can I combine services for an event?

Yes. Tell us which services interest you and we can help plan a coordinated dessert selection for your occasion.
